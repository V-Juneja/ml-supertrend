| Communication and presentation | 10/10 | Uses a five-page declaration with named equations, defined variables, clean tables and sourced charts; distinguishes observed results from model scenarios and interpretations. |

## Limits that remain explicit

The direct 30-minute replay covers 70 sessions and a selected 17-name sample, not a point-in-time universe or long-run out-of-sample period. It does not establish that adaptation adds performance; in this window the fixed-setting comparison did better. The archived ranking replay follows different rules. Judges may weigh these limits differently when assigning their score.
